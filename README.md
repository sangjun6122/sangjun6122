Research interests: 3D metrology and reconstruction, computer vision, and machine learning for industrial inspection.

## Current Research

**Reference-Guided 3D Reconstruction and Metrology of Repetitive Microstructures from Sparse Confocal Scans**

Dense confocal scanning requires many axial images at each measurement location. The proposed approach uses dense measurements of a reference structure and fewer axial images of other structures. The planned evaluation will assess height and volume errors, preservation of structure-to-structure variation, and total measurement time, including reference acquisition.

**Status:** In progress · Private repository
